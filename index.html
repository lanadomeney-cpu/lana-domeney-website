About

A voice for the moment

“Brilliant singer and performer.”M.C.

Lana Domeney is an international vocalist and entertainer, born in Essex, UK and raised in Sydney, Australia, who discovered her passion for singing at the age of eleven. Today, she is recognized among the premier singers in Sydney for corporate galas, wedding bands, and headline stage shows.

Trained in musical theatre at the McDonald College of Performing Arts and ED5International, Lana has honed her craft on both land and sea, headlining in Greece, starring as a resident vocalist on Sydney Showboats, and performing across Carnival UK & US cruise fleets.

A versatile vocalist, Lana blends Pop, Jazz, and timeless classics into a captivating live experience. Whether performing an intimate solo set or fronting full party bands in Sydney, her natural charisma and magnetic stage presence delight audiences worldwide.
