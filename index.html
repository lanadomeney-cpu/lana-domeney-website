About

A voice for the moment

“Brilliant singer and performer.”M.C.

Lana Domeney is an international vocalist and entertainer, born in Essex, UK and raised in Sydney, Australia, who discovered her passion for singing at the age of eleven.

Trained in musical theatre at the McDonald College of Performing Arts and ED5International, Lana has honed her craft on both land and sea, headlining in Greece and performing across Carnival UK & US cruise ships.

A versatile vocalist, Lana blends Pop, Jazz and timeless classics into a captivating live show. With natural charisma and magnetic stage presence, she continues to delight audiences around the world.
